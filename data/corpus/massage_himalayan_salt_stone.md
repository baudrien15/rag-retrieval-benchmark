# Himalayan Salt Stone Massage

One of the massage treatments at Lumen Spa & Wellness. This sheet covers
what the treatment involves and what it costs.

**What it is.** Hand-carved salt stones are warmed and worked along the back, arms and legs. The stones are used in constant motion rather than left in place.

**Pressure.** Medium. The warmth of the stones does much of the work, so less physical pressure is needed.

**Duration and price.** 75 minutes at €102.

**Oil and preparation.** A light unscented oil. The salt leaves a faint mineral residue on the skin, which is left rather than wiped off.

**Afterwards.** A shower is available afterwards if you would rather not leave with the residue.

**Booking note.** Not performed over broken skin or fresh shaving, as the salt will sting.

Use of the sauna, hammam and relaxation lounge on the day of the
treatment is included, as it is with every treatment we offer. The
evening and Sunday supplement that applies across the massage list
applies to this treatment too.
