# Facial treatment price list

Prices for all facial treatments at Lumen Spa & Wellness. What each
facial does, and which skin type it suits, is described separately.

| Treatment | Duration | Price |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Hydraglow Facial | 60 min | €90 |
| Clarity Peel Facial | 45 min | €70 |
| Marine Lift Facial | 75 min | €110 |

Prices are per person and include access to the sauna, hammam and
relaxation lounge on the day of your treatment.

A course of six facials of the same type is charged at five times the
single-treatment price. The course must be used within twelve months of
the first appointment.

An eye contour add-on can be attached to any facial for €20 and adds
fifteen minutes to the appointment.

Facial prices apply to the treatment only. Any products used during the
treatment are included in the price.

A supplement of €15 applies to any facial booked after 19:00 on a
weekday or at any time on Sunday.
