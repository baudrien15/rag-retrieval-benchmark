# Booking and payment

**How to book.** Online through our booking page, by phone during
opening hours, or in person at reception. Online booking closes four
hours before each slot; after that, phone us.

**Deposit.** A deposit of €30 per treatment is taken when the booking
is confirmed. It is deducted from the final price on the day. The
deposit is per treatment, so a booking for two people carries two
deposits. Deposits are refunded in full when an appointment is
cancelled within the notice period set out in our cancellation terms.

**Paying the balance.** The balance is settled at reception after your
treatment. We accept debit and credit cards, contactless payment, and
cash. We do not accept cheques.

**Gift cards.** Gift cards are accepted in payment for treatments. The
rules on what a gift card can and cannot be applied to are set out in
our gift card terms.

**Prices.** The price charged is the price in force on the day of the
treatment, not on the day of booking. If a price changes between the
two, and the change is upward, we honour the original price for
bookings already confirmed.

**Receipts.** A receipt is issued at reception for every payment, and
by email on request.
