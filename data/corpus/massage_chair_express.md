# Express Chair Massage

One of the massage treatments at Lumen Spa & Wellness. This sheet covers
what the treatment involves and what it costs.

**What it is.** A short seated treatment on a purpose-built massage chair, covering the back, shoulders and arms. Performed fully clothed in a screened corner of the relaxation lounge rather than in a treatment room.

**Pressure.** Firm, since it works through clothing.

**Duration and price.** 20 minutes at €30.

**Oil and preparation.** None.

**Afterwards.** Nothing to change out of and nothing to wash off.

**Booking note.** The only treatment we take without an appointment, subject to a therapist being free.

Use of the sauna, hammam and relaxation lounge on the day of the
treatment is included, as it is with every treatment we offer. The
evening and Sunday supplement that applies across the massage list
applies to this treatment too.
