# Facilities

**Sauna.** Dry heat, 85°C, seats eight. Two twelve-minute sessions with
a cool-down between them is the usual pattern.

**Hammam.** Steam, 45°C at high humidity, seats six. Gentler on the
breathing than the sauna and the better choice if dry heat does not
suit you.

The sauna and the hammam are our two heat facilities. Where our
suitability guidance refers to heat facilities, it means these two.

**Relaxation lounge.** Twelve reclining chairs, low light, no phones
and no conversation. Herbal tea, water and fresh fruit are served
throughout the day. This is where you wait before a treatment and where
most people stay for a while afterwards. Arriving early to sit here
before your appointment is encouraged rather than merely tolerated.

**Changing rooms.** Lockers, showers, hairdryers. Robes, slippers and
towels are provided and are included with every treatment.

**Access.** Facilities use on the day of your treatment is included in
the treatment price. Without a treatment booked, a day pass costs €25.
Members' access is set out in our membership terms.

Swimwear is required in the sauna and hammam. Bare feet only on the
wooden decking.
