# Massage treatments

What each of our massage treatments involves. Prices are listed
separately.

**Deep Tissue Massage.** Firm, slow pressure working into the deeper
muscle layers. Suited to tight shoulders, a stiff lower back, and
recovery after repeated physical effort or heavy training. It is a
working massage rather than a soothing one, and some tenderness for a
day afterwards is normal. Tell your therapist if the pressure is too
strong at any point.

**Deep Relax Aromatherapy Massage.** Despite the similar name, this is
the opposite treatment: light to medium pressure, long flowing strokes,
and a blend of essential oils chosen with you at the start of the
session. Intended for winding down rather than for muscular work.

**Hot Stone Massage.** Smooth basalt stones are warmed and placed along
the back and limbs, then used with the hands to work the muscles. The
heat carries deeper than hands alone, so the pressure feels gentler
than it is.

**Prenatal Massage.** Available from the second trimester onwards.
Performed in a side-lying position with bolster support, using
unscented oil. Pressure is kept light throughout and the abdomen and
lower back are avoided. Our prenatal therapists hold a separate
qualification for this treatment.
