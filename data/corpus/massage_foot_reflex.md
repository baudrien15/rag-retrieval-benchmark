# Foot Reflex Massage

One of the massage treatments at Lumen Spa & Wellness. This sheet covers
what the treatment involves and what it costs.

**What it is.** Pressure applied to specific points on the soles, arches and toes, working through a set sequence for each foot in turn. Only the feet and lower calves are treated.

**Pressure.** Firm and precise, held at each point rather than stroked.

**Duration and price.** 40 minutes at €58.

**Oil and preparation.** A light balm rather than oil, so the therapist's grip does not slip.

**Afterwards.** The feet often feel warm for some time afterwards. Wearing loose shoes home is more comfortable.

**Booking note.** Performed reclined and fully clothed apart from the feet, which makes it the treatment we suggest to guests who would rather not undress.

Use of the sauna, hammam and relaxation lounge on the day of the
treatment is included, as it is with every treatment we offer. The
evening and Sunday supplement that applies across the massage list
applies to this treatment too.
