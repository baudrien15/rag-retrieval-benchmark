# Body treatments

What each body treatment involves. Prices are listed separately. The
Lumen Signature Journey is described in its own document.

**Sea Salt Glow Scrub.** A coarse sea salt and oil exfoliation applied
over the whole body, worked in by hand and rinsed off under a warm
shower in the treatment room. Skin is left noticeably smoother. Not
performed on broken skin, sunburn, or within 24 hours of shaving or
waxing.

**Seaweed Wrap.** Warm algae paste is applied over the body, which is
then wrapped and left to rest for around twenty minutes while a scalp
massage is given. The wrap is rinsed off and a body cream applied.
Chosen for skin that feels dry or dull, and for a sense of heaviness in
the legs.

**Warm Oil Cocoon.** Warmed plant oil is poured and worked over the
body, which is then wrapped in a heated blanket. The gentlest of the
three, and the usual choice for anyone who finds scrubs too abrasive.

**Before you book.** Wraps and heat-based treatments are not suitable
for everyone. Suitability is set out in our contraindications guidance,
and a health form is completed on arrival. If a treatment turns out to
be unsuitable on the day, we will move you to an alternative rather
than cancel.
