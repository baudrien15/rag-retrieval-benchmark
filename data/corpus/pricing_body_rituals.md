# Body ritual price list

Prices for body treatments and multi-step rituals at Lumen Spa &
Wellness. What each ritual involves is described separately.

| Treatment | Duration | Price |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Sea Salt Glow Scrub | 45 min | €65 |
| Seaweed Wrap | 60 min | €80 |
| Warm Oil Cocoon | 60 min | €85 |
| Lumen Signature Journey | see description | €240 |

The Lumen Signature Journey is a multi-step ritual and is not priced by
the hour. Its price above is per person and covers every step. Because
the steps run in sequence with rest periods between them, the time to
allow is given in the treatment description rather than here.

Prices are per person and include access to the sauna, hammam and
relaxation lounge on the day of your treatment.

A supplement of €15 applies to any body ritual booked after 19:00 on a
weekday or at any time on Sunday. The Signature Journey is not
available after 17:00 on any day.

Body rituals involving heat or full-body wrapping are subject to
suitability checks. Ask when booking if you are unsure.
