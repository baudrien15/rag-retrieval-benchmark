# House rules

The point of these is quiet. Everything below follows from that.

**Minimum age 16.** We do not admit anyone under 16, including in the
reception area and including accompanied. This is the rule people are
most often disappointed by, and we hold to it: a single conversation at
normal volume carries through the whole floor. There is no children's
treatment list and no family session.

**Phones off.** Not silent — off, and left in your locker. This applies
to the treatment rooms, the relaxation lounge, the sauna and the
hammam. Reception will take a message and pass it to you if you are
expecting something urgent.

**No photography** anywhere beyond reception.

**Voices down.** The relaxation lounge is a silent room. Elsewhere,
speak as you would in a library.

**No alcohol.** We do not serve it and we ask that you do not arrive
having drunk. Heat and alcohol together are genuinely unsafe, and a
therapist may decline to carry out a treatment.

**Scent.** Please arrive without perfume or aftershave. It lingers in
the treatment rooms and interferes with the oils.

Guests who ignore these are asked to leave, and the treatment is
charged in full.
