# Groups and private hire

**Group bookings.** For parties of four or more, treatments are
arranged together so that everyone finishes at roughly the same time.
Book at least two weeks ahead — we can rarely fit a group in at shorter
notice, because it needs several therapists free at once.

**Private hire.** The whole spa can be taken privately. The minimum
group size for a private booking is 8 people. Below that number we
cannot cover the cost of closing to other guests.

Private hire starts at €1,200 for a three-hour slot, which covers
exclusive use, facilities, and a therapist for every two guests.
Treatments are charged on top at the usual prices.

**Notice.** Private hire needs three weeks' notice and a 50% payment at
the time of booking. The remaining balance is settled on the day.

**Timing.** A private booking must fit inside our normal opening hours
for that day, so the day of the week decides how long a slot can be. We
do not open early or stay late for private hire.

**Refreshments.** Herbal tea and fruit are included. Anything beyond
that is arranged with an outside caterer and billed separately.

**Age.** Our minimum age applies to private hire as it does to
everything else.
