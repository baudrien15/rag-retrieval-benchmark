# Swedish Classic Massage

One of the massage treatments at Lumen Spa & Wellness. This sheet covers
what the treatment involves and what it costs.

**What it is.** The standard full-body massage: long gliding strokes, kneading of the larger muscle groups, and a steady rhythm from shoulders to feet. The treatment most people picture when they think of a massage.

**Pressure.** Medium throughout, adjusted on request. Neither a deep muscular treatment nor a purely light one.

**Duration and price.** 60 minutes at €78, or 90 minutes at €108.

**Oil and preparation.** A light unscented base oil is used as standard. A scented blend can be substituted at no extra cost if you ask at the start.

**Afterwards.** You can return to a normal day straight afterwards. Drinking water in the hours that follow is advised.

**Booking note.** Available in any treatment room, so it is usually the easiest massage to fit in at short notice.

Use of the sauna, hammam and relaxation lounge on the day of the
treatment is included, as it is with every treatment we offer. The
evening and Sunday supplement that applies across the massage list
applies to this treatment too.
