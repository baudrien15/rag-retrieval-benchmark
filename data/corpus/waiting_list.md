# Waiting list and short-notice slots

**Joining.** Ask reception to put you on the list for a date and a
treatment. There is nothing to pay and nothing is held.

**How we work through it.** Strictly in the order names were added, one
call per freed slot. If you do not answer, we move to the next name
rather than leave a message and wait.

**How much notice you will get.** Usually the day before, sometimes the
same morning. A slot freed less than two hours before its start is not
offered through the list at all - it goes to whoever is in the building.

**How long a slot is held for you.** Fifteen minutes from the call. After
that it goes back out.

**Coming off the list.** Names are cleared once the date has passed. For
a treatment rather than a date, the name stays for 60 days and is then
cleared.

**Several treatments.** One name can sit on the list for more than one
treatment at once. Accepting a slot for one does not remove you from the
others.
