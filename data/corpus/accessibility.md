# Accessibility

**Getting in.** The building is entered through an archway from the
quay. The archway is level and 1.4 metres wide at its narrowest point.
There is no step at the entrance itself.

**Inside.** Reception, the relaxation lounge, the two wet rooms and four
of the dry treatment rooms are on the ground floor and are reached
without stairs. Three treatment rooms and the hammam are on the first
floor, reached by a lift 90 cm wide.

**Treatment tables.** Two of our tables lower to 48 cm for transfers.
Tell us when booking and we will assign one of those rooms.

**Hearing.** Reception has a hearing loop. Therapists are used to
working without background music if you would rather follow speech.

**Sight.** A guide dog is welcome anywhere in the building including the
relaxation lounge, which is the one exception to our rule on animals.

**Companions.** Someone accompanying you who is not being treated is
welcome to wait in the relaxation lounge at no charge, provided they are
over our minimum age like any other guest.

**Telling us.** None of this needs explaining on the day. A note when
booking is enough, and it means the right room is held from the start.
