# Twilight Evening package

One of the spa day packages at Lumen Spa & Wellness. A package is booked
as a whole and covers the facilities, the treatment and the food listed
below.

**Hours.** 18:00 to 21:00

**Price.** €98 per person

**Treatment included.** one 30-minute treatment of your choice

**Food included.** a small evening plate

**Facilities.** Unlimited use of the sauna, hammam and relaxation lounge
throughout the booked hours, before and after the treatment.

**Note.** The evening supplement is built into the package price and is not added again.

**Extra treatments.** Anything booked beyond what the package includes
is charged at the usual single-treatment price for that treatment.

**Arrival.** Come 15 minutes before the start of the package. The hours
above are the hours of the package itself, not of your arrival.

Spa day packages are covered by their own cancellation terms rather than
by the standard ones.
