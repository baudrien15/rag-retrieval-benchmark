# Massage price list

Prices for all massage treatments at Lumen Spa & Wellness. Treatment
descriptions, including what each massage involves and how it is
performed, are held separately.

| Treatment | Duration | Price |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Deep Tissue Massage | 60 min | €85 |
| Deep Tissue Massage | 90 min | €115 |
| Deep Relax Aromatherapy Massage | 60 min | €80 |
| Deep Relax Aromatherapy Massage | 90 min | €110 |
| Hot Stone Massage | 75 min | €95 |
| Prenatal Massage | 60 min | €75 |

Prices are per person and include access to the sauna, hammam and
relaxation lounge on the day of your treatment.

A supplement of €15 applies to any massage booked after 19:00 on a
weekday or at any time on Sunday.

Two people can be treated in the same room on request. There is no
additional charge for this, but it must be arranged when booking, as
only two of our treatment rooms are large enough.

Massage treatments cannot be combined with facial or body ritual
pricing. Where a package includes more than one treatment type, the
package price applies instead.
