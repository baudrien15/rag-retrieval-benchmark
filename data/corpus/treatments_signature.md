# The Lumen Signature Journey

Our flagship ritual, and the only treatment that combines all three of
our treatment families in one appointment. The price is given in the
body ritual price list.

**The four steps, in order.**

1. Sea Salt Glow Scrub, followed by a warm shower.
2. A rest period in the relaxation lounge with herbal tea.
3. A 60-minute massage. You choose between the Deep Relax
   Aromatherapy Massage and the Deep Tissue Massage when you book.
4. The Hydraglow Facial, finishing with a scalp and shoulder massage.

**How long to allow.** Three hours and fifteen minutes from arrival to
departure. Two hours and forty minutes of that is treatment time; the
rest is the rest period between steps and time to change. We do not
shorten the Journey to fit a smaller window, because the rest period
between the scrub and the massage is part of the treatment rather than
a gap in it.

**Practical points.** Arrive as you would for any appointment. The
Journey is performed by two therapists working in sequence, so it
cannot be moved once booked without rearranging both of their days —
changes need more notice than a single treatment.

The Journey is not suitable during pregnancy, as it includes a scrub
and a facial in combination with prolonged heat.
