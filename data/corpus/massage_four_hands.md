# Four Hands Massage

One of the massage treatments at Lumen Spa & Wellness. This sheet covers
what the treatment involves and what it costs.

**What it is.** Two therapists working simultaneously in mirrored movements across the body. The effect is disorientating in a way a single-therapist massage is not, because the mind cannot track both pairs of hands.

**Pressure.** Medium, matched between the two therapists throughout.

**Duration and price.** 50 minutes at €150. The price reflects two therapists for the full session.

**Oil and preparation.** A single scented blend is chosen at the start and used by both therapists.

**Afterwards.** Most guests want to sit quietly afterwards rather than leave immediately.

**Booking note.** Needs two therapists free at the same time, so it must be booked further ahead than any other massage on our list.

Use of the sauna, hammam and relaxation lounge on the day of the
treatment is included, as it is with every treatment we offer. The
evening and Sunday supplement that applies across the massage list
applies to this treatment too.
