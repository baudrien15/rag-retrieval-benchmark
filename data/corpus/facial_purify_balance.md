# Purify Balance Facial

One of the facial treatments at Lumen Spa & Wellness. This sheet covers
what the facial does and what it costs.

**Who it suits.** Skin that runs oily through the t-zone and congests around the nose and chin.

**What happens.** Double cleanse, steam, manual extraction where needed, a clay mask, then a light non-oily moisturiser.

**Duration and price.** 60 minutes at €84.

**Afterwards.** Some redness around any extraction site for an hour or two. Makeup is best left off until the evening.

**Consultation.** Every facial begins with a short look at your skin
under a lamp, so the therapist can confirm the treatment is the right
one before starting. If it is not, we will say so and change it rather
than proceed.

Use of the sauna, hammam and relaxation lounge on the day of the
treatment is included, as it is with every treatment we offer. The
evening and Sunday supplement that applies across the facial list
applies to this treatment too.
