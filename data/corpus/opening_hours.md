**Last appointment.** The last treatment of the day starts 90 minutes
before we close, and longer treatments start earlier still — a
90-minute massage on a Sunday must begin by 15:30.

**Facilities.** The sauna and hammam stop taking new entries 45 minutes
before closing so that they can be cleaned. The relaxation lounge stays
open until closing.

**Closures.** We are closed on 25 December and 1 January. On 24 and 31
December we close at 16:00. We are open on all other public holidays,
on Sunday hours.

**Quiet times.** Weekday mornings before 11:00 are the quietest period
and the easiest time to get a late booking. Saturdays are usually full
two to three weeks ahead.

Reception answers the phone from 30 minutes after opening until 30
minutes before closing.
