# Who should avoid what

Everyone completes a short health form on arrival. This guidance sets
out the main restrictions in advance so that you can book something
suitable. It describes who should avoid which treatment. It is not
medical advice, and we do not assess symptoms or tell you what a
symptom means — for that, see your doctor.

**Pregnancy.** No hot stone treatment and no full-body wrap at any
stage. Prolonged heat is not recommended, which rules out our heat
facilities. Massage is possible from the second trimester, but only the
one treatment on our massage list that is written for it — the massage
treatment descriptions say which, and how it differs.

**After surgery.** Wait eight weeks after any surgical procedure before
booking a treatment involving heat, and before using any of our heat
facilities — the list of which is given in our facilities guidance.
Massage over or near a scar needs six months and your surgeon's
agreement.

**Heart conditions and blood pressure.** Prolonged heat places load on
the circulation. If you are treated for a heart condition or for high
or low blood pressure, avoid heat treatments and heat facilities, and
tell your therapist before any massage.

**Allergies.** Several of our oils are nut-based and our algae products
are derived from shellfish habitats. Tell us at booking, not on
arrival, so that an alternative can be prepared.

**Recent injury, fever, infection.** Postpone until you have recovered.
