# Shiatsu Massage

One of the massage treatments at Lumen Spa & Wellness. This sheet covers
what the treatment involves and what it costs.

**What it is.** Pressure applied through the thumbs, elbows and knees along the body's meridian lines, performed on a padded futon at floor level rather than on a table.

**Pressure.** Firm and static - pressure is held at a point rather than glided across the muscle.

**Duration and price.** 60 minutes at €88.

**Oil and preparation.** None. The treatment is performed fully clothed.

**Afterwards.** Wear something you can move in. Tight jeans make the stretches awkward.

**Booking note.** We have one futon room, so the times available for this treatment are more limited than for our table massages.

Use of the sauna, hammam and relaxation lounge on the day of the
treatment is included, as it is with every treatment we offer. The
evening and Sunday supplement that applies across the massage list
applies to this treatment too.
