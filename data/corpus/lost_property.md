# Lost property

**How long we keep things.** Anything found is held for 30 days from the
day it was found. After 30 days, unclaimed items are given to a local
charity, apart from documents and cards, which are destroyed.

**Valuables.** Jewellery, watches, phones and cards are logged and held
in the safe at reception rather than in the general lost property box.

**Claiming.** Describe the item before we show it to you. We will not
produce a tray of found items and ask you to pick.

**Posting things on.** We will post an item within France at your cost,
paid by card over the phone. We do not post outside France and we do not
post anything held in the safe.

**Clothing left in a locker.** Lockers are emptied every evening after
closing and anything left goes into lost property under the same 30-day
rule. Nothing is held in the locker overnight.

**Things we do not keep.** Water bottles, single socks, and anything
that has been left more than once by the same guest in the same month
are disposed of at closing rather than logged, which saves everyone the
conversation.
