# Requesting a therapist

**By name.** You can ask for a particular therapist when booking and we
will hold the appointment with them. There is no charge for this.

**What it costs you in choice.** Asking for one therapist narrows the
times available, sometimes considerably. Where the same treatment is
free two hours earlier with a colleague, we will say so, but the choice
stays yours.

**Male or female.** You can ask for a male or a female therapist without
naming anyone, and you do not need to give a reason. Our team is mixed
and this can almost always be met.

**If your therapist is away.** Where the therapist you asked for is ill
on the day, we will call and offer a colleague or a different date. We
do not substitute silently.

**During the treatment.** If at any point you would rather stop, say so
and the treatment stops. What has been taken so far is charged; nothing
further is.

**Training therapists.** Some appointments are held by therapists
completing their final supervised hours. Where that is the case, we say
so at the time of booking and never on the day.
