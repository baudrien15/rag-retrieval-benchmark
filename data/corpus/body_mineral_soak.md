# Mineral Bath Soak

One of the body treatments at Lumen Spa & Wellness. This sheet covers
what the treatment involves and what it costs.

**What happens.** A private mineral bath drawn at 37 degrees, taken alone, with the lights low and the room to yourself.

**Duration and price.** 30 minutes at €40.

**Time spent wrapped.** Not a wrap treatment and not a hands-on treatment - no therapist is present.

**Showering.** A shower is available in the same room afterwards.

**What to wear.** Disposable underwear is provided and worn throughout.
Bring nothing of your own for this treatment.

**Room.** Body treatments are performed in one of our two wet rooms,
which have a shower and a heated table. There are fewer of these rooms
than dry treatment rooms, so appointment times are more limited.

Use of the sauna, hammam and relaxation lounge on the day of the
treatment is included, as it is with every treatment we offer. The
evening and Sunday supplement that applies across the body treatment
list applies to this treatment too.
