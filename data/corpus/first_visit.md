# Your first visit

Most people have not been to a spa before and are unsure what is
expected. Nothing here is complicated.

**Arrive 15 minutes early.** This is not padding. You need to change,
complete a short health form, and sit down for a few minutes — arriving
straight from the street with your pulse up undoes part of the
treatment. If you are running late, our cancellation and late arrival
terms explain what happens.

**Bring swimwear** if you intend to use the sauna or hammam. Robes,
slippers and towels are provided, so there is nothing else to bring.

**Leave jewellery at home.** There are lockers, but the simplest
approach is not to bring anything you would mind losing.

**Undressing.** For a massage you undress to your underwear. You are
covered by a towel throughout and only the area being worked on is
uncovered. For a facial you stay dressed from the waist down. Nobody
will ask you to be more exposed than you are comfortable with.

**Talking.** You do not have to make conversation. Say so at the start
and your therapist will work in silence.

**Afterwards.** Drink water and give yourself half an hour before doing
anything demanding. The relaxation lounge is there for exactly this.
