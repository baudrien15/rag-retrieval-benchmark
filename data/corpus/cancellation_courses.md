# Cancelling treatment courses

These terms govern a single appointment within a prepaid course of treatments. They replace the standard cancellation
terms for this type of booking only. An ordinary individual treatment is
not covered here and follows the standard terms instead.

**Notice required.** 48 hours before the booked date.

**Cancelling with less notice.** the session, which is deducted from the course as though it had been taken is charged.

**Not turning up.** Where nothing is said to us at all, the session, deducted from the course is
charged.

**How to cancel.** By phone or by replying to the confirmation email.
As with any booking, a message left outside opening hours is not read
until the following morning, and the notice period runs from when we
read it rather than from when it was sent.

**Changing rather than cancelling.** Cancelling the remainder of a course is a separate matter: unused sessions are refunded at the single-treatment price already paid, less any discount the course carried.

**When we cancel.** If we cancel, nothing is charged and the payment is
returned in full within five working days. The reduction we offer on a
rebooked treatment applies here as it does to any booking we cancel.
