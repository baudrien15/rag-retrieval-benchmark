# Scalp and Neck Massage

One of the massage treatments at Lumen Spa & Wellness. This sheet covers
what the treatment involves and what it costs.

**What it is.** A seated treatment covering the scalp, neck, shoulders and upper back. Clothing stays on from the waist down and the treatment is performed through a light cotton cover on the shoulders.

**Pressure.** Medium on the shoulders, light and circular on the scalp.

**Duration and price.** 30 minutes at €48.

**Oil and preparation.** No oil on the scalp unless you ask for it. A small amount is used on the neck and shoulders.

**Afterwards.** You can go straight back to work. Nothing is left in the hair if the oil-free option is chosen.

**Booking note.** The shortest table treatment we run, and the one most often booked in a lunch hour.

Use of the sauna, hammam and relaxation lounge on the day of the
treatment is included, as it is with every treatment we offer. The
evening and Sunday supplement that applies across the massage list
applies to this treatment too.
