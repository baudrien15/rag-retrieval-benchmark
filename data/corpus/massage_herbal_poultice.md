# Herbal Poultice Massage

One of the massage treatments at Lumen Spa & Wellness. This sheet covers
what the treatment involves and what it costs.

**What it is.** Muslin bundles of dried herbs are steamed until hot, then pressed and rolled over the muscles between passes of hand massage.

**Pressure.** Medium, alternating between the poultice and the hands.

**Duration and price.** 80 minutes at €118.

**Oil and preparation.** A warmed unscented oil beneath, with the poultice supplying the scent.

**Afterwards.** The scent stays on the skin for several hours and is stronger than most guests expect.

**Booking note.** Each poultice is used for a single treatment and then discarded, which is reflected in the price.

Use of the sauna, hammam and relaxation lounge on the day of the
treatment is included, as it is with every treatment we offer. The
evening and Sunday supplement that applies across the massage list
applies to this treatment too.
