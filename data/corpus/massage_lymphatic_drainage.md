# Lymphatic Drainage Massage

One of the massage treatments at Lumen Spa & Wellness. This sheet covers
what the treatment involves and what it costs.

**What it is.** Very light, repetitive strokes following the lymphatic pathways, working from the extremities inwards. It is a slow treatment and deliberately undramatic.

**Pressure.** Light throughout. The pressure is far lighter than a standard massage and is not increased on request, as the technique depends on it.

**Duration and price.** 50 minutes at €82, or 80 minutes at €112.

**Oil and preparation.** Performed on bare skin without oil, or with a trace of unscented balm where the skin is dry.

**Afterwards.** A noticeable increase in trips to the bathroom over the following day is expected and normal.

**Booking note.** Requires a therapist with the specific qualification, so the choice of appointment times is narrower than for other massages.

Use of the sauna, hammam and relaxation lounge on the day of the
treatment is included, as it is with every treatment we offer. The
evening and Sunday supplement that applies across the massage list
applies to this treatment too.
