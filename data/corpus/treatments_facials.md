# Facial treatments

What each of our facials does, and who it suits. Prices are listed
separately.

**Hydraglow Facial.** Our hydration facial. A gentle enzyme cleanse,
followed by a hyaluronic serum and a cooling gel mask. Aimed at skin
that looks tired, flat or lacking radiance, which is usually a
hydration problem rather than a cleansing one. This is the facial most
often chosen before an event, as the effect is immediate and there is
no visible reaction afterwards.

**Clarity Peel Facial.** A light acid peel for congested skin,
blocked pores and uneven texture. Mild redness for two to three hours
afterwards is normal. Not suitable in the week before an event, nor if
you have had significant sun exposure in the previous ten days.

**Marine Lift Facial.** A firming treatment using an algae-based mask
and a manual lifting massage of the face, neck and décolleté. Aimed at
loss of firmness along the jawline and cheekbones.

**Choosing between them.** If you are unsure, our therapists carry out
a short skin assessment at the start of every facial appointment and
will change the treatment if a different one suits you better. There is
no charge for switching, though the price of the treatment actually
performed applies.
