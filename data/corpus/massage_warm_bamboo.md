# Warm Bamboo Massage

One of the massage treatments at Lumen Spa & Wellness. This sheet covers
what the treatment involves and what it costs.

**What it is.** Heated bamboo canes of several diameters are rolled and pressed along the muscles, used in place of the therapist's forearms for the broader strokes.

**Pressure.** Firm. The canes concentrate pressure more than a hand does, so the same effort travels deeper.

**Duration and price.** 70 minutes at €96.

**Oil and preparation.** A warmed unscented oil, applied generously so the canes glide rather than drag.

**Afterwards.** Some warmth in the treated muscles for a few hours afterwards is normal.

**Booking note.** Not suitable over recent bruising, as the canes cannot work around it the way hands can.

Use of the sauna, hammam and relaxation lounge on the day of the
treatment is included, as it is with every treatment we offer. The
evening and Sunday supplement that applies across the massage list
applies to this treatment too.
