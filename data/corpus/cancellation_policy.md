# Cancellation and late arrival

These terms apply to every booked treatment.

**Cancelling.** Cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment and
there is nothing to pay. Cancel less than 24 hours before and 50% of
the treatment price is charged. If you do not come at all and do not
tell us, the full treatment price is charged.

**How to cancel.** By phone, or through the link in your confirmation
email. We cannot accept a cancellation left as a voicemail outside
opening hours, because the message is not read until the following
morning — if the appointment is that evening, it counts as a no-show.

**Arriving late.** Your treatment ends at the time it was scheduled to
end, whatever time you arrive. A therapist's day is booked back to
back, so lost minutes cannot be added on. The full price is charged for
a shortened treatment.

**More than 20 minutes late.** The appointment is treated as a no-show
and the full price is charged. We will always try to fit you in later
the same day instead, but we cannot promise it.

**When we cancel.** If we cancel — therapist illness, a facilities
problem — you are charged nothing and we offer the next available
equivalent slot at a 20% reduction.
