# Finding us

Lumen Spa & Wellness
18 Quai des Tanneurs
44000 Nantes

The entrance is set back from the quay, under the stone archway between
the bookshop and the flower stall. There is no sign on the quay itself,
which catches most people out the first time.

**By tram.** Line 1 to the Tanneurs stop, then four minutes on foot
along the quay heading north. This is the simplest way to reach us.

**By bus.** Route 26 stops at Pont Sauvetout, six minutes away on foot.

**By bicycle.** Racks are fixed to the wall inside the archway, within
sight of reception. A shared-bike station sits at the far end of the
quay, about 100 metres away.

**By car.** There is no parking at the building and the quay itself is
permit-only. The nearest public car park is underground beneath Place
Duchesne, 200 metres away, and is paid at all hours. Traffic along the
quay is slow on Saturday afternoons — allow more time than the map
suggests.

**Step-free access.** The archway and reception are step-free. Two
treatment rooms and one changing room are on the ground floor; the
remainder are reached by a lift.
