# Thai Oil Massage

One of the massage treatments at Lumen Spa & Wellness. This sheet covers
what the treatment involves and what it costs.

**What it is.** Assisted stretching combined with pressure along the body's energy lines, performed with oil on a treatment table rather than on a floor mat.

**Pressure.** Firm, applied with thumbs, palms and forearms, and punctuated by held stretches.

**Duration and price.** 75 minutes at €98. There is no shorter version - the sequence does not compress.

**Oil and preparation.** A warmed unscented oil is used throughout.

**Afterwards.** Expect to feel loose rather than sleepy. Some people find their range of movement noticeably better for a day or two.

**Booking note.** Loose clothing is not needed, as the treatment is done with oil under a towel.

Use of the sauna, hammam and relaxation lounge on the day of the
treatment is included, as it is with every treatment we offer. The
evening and Sunday supplement that applies across the massage list
applies to this treatment too.
