# Cancelling private hire

These terms govern an exclusive-use booking of the whole spa. They replace the standard cancellation
terms for this type of booking only. An ordinary individual treatment is
not covered here and follows the standard terms instead.

**Notice required.** 14 days before the booked date. This is the
notice to cancel a booking that already exists. The separate and longer
notice needed to make a private hire booking in the first place is set
out with the private hire terms, not here.

**Cancelling with less notice.** the booking payment already made, which is not returned is charged.

**Not turning up.** Where nothing is said to us at all, the full quoted price of the slot is
charged.

**How to cancel.** By phone or by replying to the confirmation email.
As with any booking, a message left outside opening hours is not read
until the following morning, and the notice period runs from when we
read it rather than from when it was sent.

**Changing rather than cancelling.** A private hire date can be moved once, free of charge, if we are told more than 14 days ahead and the new date falls within three months.

**When we cancel.** If we cancel, nothing is charged and the payment is
returned in full within five working days. The reduction we offer on a
rebooked treatment applies here as it does to any booking we cancel.
