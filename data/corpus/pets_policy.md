# Animals

**The rule.** Animals are not admitted to any part of the building. This
includes reception, the relaxation lounge, the treatment rooms and the
courtyard behind the archway.

**The exception.** A guide dog or other assistance dog is welcome
throughout, and needs no notice, no documentation and no arrangement in
advance. Simply arrive.

**Why the rule exists.** Several of our treatments are performed on bare
skin in rooms that are cleaned between guests to a standard that animal
hair makes impossible to hold. It is not a preference.

**Waiting outside.** There is nowhere on the quay to leave an animal
safely, and the archway is a shared entrance we do not control. We would
rather say plainly that there is no arrangement than suggest one that
does not work.

**Someone else's animal.** If you are visiting with a companion who has
an animal with them, the same applies - the animal cannot come in, and
the companion cannot wait inside with it.

**Assistance dogs in the wet rooms.** The wet rooms have a heated floor
that reaches 30 degrees. We will bring a mat so the dog can settle
somewhere cooler.
