# Gift cards

A gift card is the usual way to give a treatment to someone else
without booking it for them — they choose the treatment and the date
themselves.

**Validity.** A gift card is valid for 12 months from the date of
purchase. The date is printed on the card. We cannot extend a card
after it has expired, and an expired card has no remaining value.

**Amounts.** Cards are sold in fixed amounts of €50, €100 and €200. A
card can also be bought for a named treatment, in which case it remains
valid for that treatment even if the price rises during the twelve
months.

**Using one.** A gift card can be used to pay for a treatment in full
or in part. If the treatment costs more than the card, the difference
is paid at reception. If it costs less, the remaining balance stays on
the card until it expires.

**What a card cannot be used for.** A gift card cannot be used to pay a
booking deposit. The deposit is taken at the moment of booking and must
be paid by card or in cash; the gift card is then applied against the
balance on the day.

**Transfer and refunds.** Cards are transferable — whoever holds the
card may use it. Cards are not refundable and cannot be exchanged for
cash.
