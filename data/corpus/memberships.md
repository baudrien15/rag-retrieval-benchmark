# Memberships

Two monthly memberships, both billed by direct debit on the first of
the month.

**Serenity — €79 per month.** One 60-minute treatment of your choice
each month, plus unlimited use of the sauna, hammam and relaxation
lounge on any day we are open, with or without an appointment.

**Radiance — €139 per month.** Two 60-minute treatments each month,
unlimited facilities access, and 10% off any further treatment booked
in the same month.

**Included treatments.** The monthly treatment is any standard
60-minute massage, facial or body treatment. Multi-step rituals are not
included, but members may put the value of their monthly treatment
towards one.

**Unused and cancelled sessions.** A monthly treatment does not carry
over — an unused session is lost at the end of the month. A session
cancelled at short notice counts as used for that month, and the
standard cancellation charge applies on top.

**Minimum term and cancelling.** Three months minimum, then 30 days'
written notice at any time. Notice given part-way through a month takes
effect at the end of the following month. There is no fee for
cancelling the membership itself.

**Freezing.** A membership can be frozen once a year for up to two
months, for medical reasons.
